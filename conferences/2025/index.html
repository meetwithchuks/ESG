About this edition

ESG Conference Nigeria 2025

The ESG Conference is the foremost gathering of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) for knowledge sharing on sustainability in Nigeria.

The maiden event convened leading experts, practitioners and ecosystem enablers to share pathways for integrating environmental, social and governance (ESG) practices at SMEs.

Conversations on stage reflected the wider sustainability agenda, the key challenges and opportunities on the road to energy transition, net-zero and more sustainable business operating models for SMEs.

Agenda highlights

The themes around which the day's sessions are organised.

Climate Action & Net-Zero Strategies

Accelerating the path to net-zero through decarbonisation strategies, climate resilience planning, and corporate sustainability commitments.

Sustainable Finance & Impact Investing

Mobilizing capital for ESG-driven growth by Nigerian companies through existing credit products and SME-targeted funds.

Circular Economy & Sustainable Supply Chains

Transforming industries by reducing waste, optimising resource efficiency, and driving ethical sourcing and sustainable production practices.

Who attends, and why

Who’ll be there

  • Chief Executive Officers (CEO)
  • Chief Financial Officers (CFO)
  • Chief Sustainability Officers (CSO)
  • Chief Operating Officers (COO)
  • Corporate Governance Analysts
  • Environmental Compliance Officers
  • Investment Managers
  • Development Partners
  • Academia

Why attend

  • Listen to the most impactful ways of incorporating ESG principles and sustainability thinking into your business practices
  • Network with other attendees to facilitate the sharing of ideas and experiences
  • Gain awareness of the importance of sustainability and ESG
  • Discover how the Bank of Industry is supporting SMEs to adopt ESG practices through targeted credit products and tailored advisory services
  • Get inspiration to be part of the vanguard of companies implementing sustainable practices and taking action on responsible business commitments
  • Learn about federal government initiatives and intervention funds to accelerate ESG adoption in Nigeria
  • Find out about funding and training opportunities from BOI, development partners, and non-governmental ecosystem enablers for supporting ESG adoption
