About this edition

BOI Sustainability Conference 2026

The BOI Sustainability Conference is the Bank of Industry's flagship platform for advancing sustainable business practice across Nigerian enterprises. The 2026 theme, Green, Resilient & Profitable, makes the case that sustainability is how Nigerian businesses stay competitive, not a cost of compliance.

The second edition opens with two keynotes — one from the Climate Investment Funds at the World Bank Group, one from AFD — and three launches: the Green Compendium, the Sustainability Recognition Awards, and the 2026 BOI–LBS State of ESG Adoption by Nigerian MSMEs report, which tracks progress since 2025.

The room then splits. Six concurrent sessions run across two blocks, each pairing a panel with a master class and a fireside conversation, so delegates choose between the capital conversation, the practitioner's workshop and the operator's story.

Date and venue will be published on this page as soon as they are confirmed.

Who attends, and why

Who’ll be there

  • Chief Executive Officers (CEO)
  • Chief Financial Officers (CFO)
  • Chief Sustainability Officers (CSO)
  • Chief Operating Officers (COO)
  • Corporate Governance Analysts
  • Environmental Compliance Officers
  • Investment Managers
  • Development Partners
  • Academia

Why attend

  • Learn practical ways to make your business greener, more resilient and more profitable
  • Understand how climate risk reaches your balance sheet, and how to manage it
  • Network with other attendees to facilitate the sharing of ideas and experiences
  • Discover how the Bank of Industry is supporting SMEs on their sustainability journey through targeted credit products and tailored advisory services
  • Get inspiration to be part of the vanguard of companies implementing sustainable practices and taking action on responsible business commitments
  • Learn about federal government initiatives and intervention funds accelerating the sustainability transition in Nigeria
  • Find out about funding and training opportunities from BOI, development partners and non-governmental ecosystem enablers for sustainable businesses
