Join us at the BOI ESG Conference 2025

The Bank of Industry invites business leaders, policymakers, investors and sustainability practitioners to its flagship ESG Conference at the Federal Palace Hotel, Lagos.

The Bank of Industry is pleased to invite you to the BOI ESG Conference 2025 — a flagship platform dedicated to advancing Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) adoption across Nigerian enterprises.

Convening under the theme "Building Resilient and Responsible Enterprises: ESG as a Strategic Imperative for Nigerian SMEs", this year's edition focuses on practical strategies for embedding sustainability into business operations while driving sustainable growth.

Who will be in the room

The conference brings together business executives, government officials, investment professionals and sustainability specialists to exchange insights and examine the methods behind a more accountable commercial sector.

What the platform is for

The BOI ESG Conference is a knowledge and engagement platform of the Bank of Industry that promotes the integration of ESG principles within Nigeria's business ecosystem. It is designed as a catalyst for dialogue and collaborative action, enabling participants to explore approaches to responsible expansion, sustainable financial practices and equitable industrial progress.

Details

  • Date: Tuesday, 28 October 2025
  • Time: 8:30 AM – 2:30 PM
  • Venue: Federal Palace Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos

We look forward to welcoming you.
