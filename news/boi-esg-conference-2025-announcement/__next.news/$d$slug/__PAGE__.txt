4:T5bd,<p>The Bank of Industry is pleased to invite you to the <strong>BOI ESG Conference
2025</strong> — a flagship platform dedicated to advancing Environmental, Social and
Governance (ESG) adoption across Nigerian enterprises.</p>
<p>Convening under the theme <strong>&quot;Building Resilient and Responsible Enterprises: ESG
as a Strategic Imperative for Nigerian SMEs&quot;</strong>, this year&#39;s edition focuses on
practical strategies for embedding sustainability into business operations while
driving sustainable growth.</p>
<h2>Who will be in the room</h2>
<p>The conference brings together business executives, government officials,
investment professionals and sustainability specialists to exchange insights and
examine the methods behind a more accountable commercial sector.</p>
<h2>What the platform is for</h2>
<p>The BOI ESG Conference is a knowledge and engagement platform of the Bank of
Industry that promotes the integration of ESG principles within Nigeria&#39;s
business ecosystem. It is designed as a catalyst for dialogue and collaborative
action, enabling participants to explore approaches to responsible expansion,
sustainable financial practices and equitable industrial progress.</p>
<h2>Details</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Date:</strong> Tuesday, 28 October 2025</li>
<li><strong>Time:</strong> 8:30 AM – 2:30 PM</li>
<li><strong>Venue:</strong> Federal Palace Hotel, Victoria Island, Lagos</li>
</ul>
